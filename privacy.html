3. Information collected automatically

This site uses a first-party analytics tool (a Cloudflare Worker I run myself - not Google Analytics or any ad network) to understand visitor traffic. It only runs after you accept the cookie banner. Once accepted, it may collect:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device type (mobile/desktop)
  • Referring page and pages viewed on this site
  • Approximate location (country/city, derived from your IP address)
  • Session timing (arrival time, time spent) and a random session identifier stored in your browser's session storage
  • Campaign tags in the link you arrived from (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content), only if the link contains them. They are remembered for the length of your visit so they can be tied to the pages you view.
  • Clicks on outbound links (such as Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube or club websites) and downloads of the PDF on the Academic page, recorded as the link text, the destination address, and the page you came from

This data is used only to see which pages and countries visitors come from. It is never sold, and never shared with advertisers or third parties for marketing.

Your IP address itself is never stored. It is used only for the moment of the request to look up your approximate country/city and to compute a one-way cryptographic hash, which is what's saved - the hash is computed with a secret key, so it cannot be turned back into your IP address. Because it can still recognise a returning visitor, it is treated as personal data. That hash is what's used to work out whether a visit is "returning" (an address associated with more than one visit) and how many people are viewing the site live.

5. Legal basis for processing

Where EU/UK/Serbian data protection law applies, the legal basis for the analytics described above is your consent, given via the cookie banner. You can withdraw that consent at any time - see Section 8.

6. Who this data passes through

No visitor data is sold to anyone. A few outside services are involved in simply running the site, and each may process a visitor's IP address as an unavoidable part of how the web works:

  • Cloudflare - hosts the Worker that receives analytics events and supplies the aggregate statistics shown on the Visitors page. That page contacts the Worker whenever it is opened, whatever your cookie choice, so Cloudflare sees your IP address for that request like any other web request
  • GitHub Pages - hosts the website files themselves
  • YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) - only if you press play on the highlight video on the Basketball page. Nothing is requested from YouTube before that click. Once the video plays, YouTube/Google receive your IP address and may store data in your browser, under their own privacy policy
  • Esri / OpenStreetMap - supplies the map tiles on the Visitors page

7. International transfers

The services above are based in or operate infrastructure in the United States and other countries. Where required, these providers rely on their own compliance mechanisms (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) for transferring data outside the EU/UK.

8. How long data is kept

Analytics events are automatically and permanently deleted after 365 days. A failed-login counter used to slow down password-guessing on the analytics dashboard is kept for 1 day.
