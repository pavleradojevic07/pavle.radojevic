Do you need to accept anything?

Yes for one category. The two items marked Necessary below are used no matter what, since the site can't remember your theme or your cookie choice without them. The items marked Optional only run after you click Accept on the banner — if you click Decline, none of them run.

Other requests that aren't cookies, but still involve a data transfer

These load automatically when a page opens — separately from the cookie banner above — because the page needs them to render fonts, maps, or 3D graphics. They can see your IP address as a normal side effect of your browser fetching a file from their servers:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — delivers this site's typefaces
  • unpkg.com — delivers the Leaflet.js and Three.js code libraries
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap (basemaps.cartocdn.com) — supplies map tile images on the Visitors page
  • threejs.org — supplies the Earth texture image on the Atlas globe

None of these set cookies on this site, and none of them are analytics — but they are a good-practice item worth knowing about. The most common fix, if you want to remove this entirely, is self-hosting the fonts and libraries instead of loading them from a third-party CDN.
