Courses

💡 Programming 2
Completed
Building on Programming 1, this course covers advanced programming techniques in C. Students learn about pointers, dynamic memory allocation, and complex data structures including linked lists, stacks, queues, and trees. The curriculum includes file handling, bitwise operators, and command-line arguments. Students explore algorithms for searching and sorting, both iterative and recursive implementations, and gain understanding of computational complexity and algorithmic undecidability.
💻 Programming 1
Completed
This course introduces students to fundamental concepts of procedural programming. Students learn basic programming constructs including data types, control structures, functions, and arrays. The course covers algorithm development, recursion, and elementary data structures, with practical implementation in the C programming language. Students also gain understanding of testing, debugging, and the historical context of computer science.
🧠 Introduction to Mathematical Logic
Completed
This course provides foundational knowledge in mathematical logic and set theory. Students learn about sets, relations, functions, cardinality, and the concept of countability. The curriculum covers propositional logic with semantics and normal forms, as well as first-order predicate logic with quantifiers. Students understand key theorems including Gödel's completeness theorem and the compactness theorem, preparing them for advanced mathematical courses.
📐 Elementary Mathematics
Completed
This course aims to standardize students' mathematical knowledge from previous education. It covers algebraic expressions, absolute values, exponentiation, logarithms, and trigonometric functions. Students work with functions, inverse functions, and their graphs, as well as solving various types of equations and inequalities. The content ensures students have mastered the mathematics taught in Serbian primary and secondary schools.
📊 Linear Algebra
Completed
This course provides advanced knowledge of linear algebra over two semesters. Students learn about vector spaces, linear mappings, bases, dimension, and matrices. The curriculum covers determinants, systems of linear equations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and diagonalization. Additional topics include bilinear and quadratic forms, Euclidean vector spaces, orthogonal transformations, and Hermitian spaces. Students gain the ability to solve problems and apply linear algebra in other mathematical contexts.
🇬🇧 English for Mathematical Purposes
Completed
This course develops students' English language skills with a focus on mathematical contexts. Students improve reading comprehension of technical texts, writing skills for professional communication, and overall language proficiency. The curriculum covers essential English grammar including verb tenses, conditionals, reported speech, and passive voice. Students learn to communicate effectively in English and independently use English-language mathematical literature.
